Hippocampal Index Theory

[Preprint]
Surya Narayanan Hari

A preprint exploring the index theory of hippocampal function. This research investigates how the hippocampus organizes and retrieves memories through a computational framework that models memory indexing and pattern completion processes in the brain.

The theory proposes that the hippocampus acts as an efficient indexing system, storing pointers to memory components distributed throughout the neocortex rather than storing entire memories itself.
