Ideal Ratio of Pasta Sauce to Pasta

[Preprint]
Surya Narayanan Hari

A scientific exploration of the perfect pasta-to-sauce ratio. This groundbreaking research examines the optimal balance between sauce and pasta for maximum flavor and texture enjoyment across different pasta shapes and sauce types.

The study considers factors such as sauce viscosity, pasta surface area, and absorption rates to determine mathematically optimal ratios for various pasta-sauce combinations.
